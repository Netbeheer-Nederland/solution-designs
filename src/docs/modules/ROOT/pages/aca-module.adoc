= ACA Insight: Waiting List, Flexibility and Load Profiles

The *DSO-TSO Congestion Data Exchange* outlines the framework for data exchange
between Distribution System Operators (DSOs) and Transmission System Operator
(TSO) for _congestion management study_ purposes. This initiative aims to
enhance the accuracy and efficiency of energy data management, ensuring
seamless integration and communication between system operators.

This document details various aspects of data exchange, including load
profiles, and prognosis logic. It emphasizes the importance of adhering to
standardized formats for time series data, which ensures consistency and
reliability in data delivery for all parties involved. The document also
highlights the roles and responsibilities of different stakeholders involved in
the project, providing a clear approach for implementation and approval
processes.

This document is prepared in English in order to facilitate software
development and ensure the broadest possible understanding and compatibility
across teams and stakeholders.

== Scope

=== Purpose

This use case provides information that supports the assessment of transport capacity demand against expected network utilisation and available flexibility.

The use case enables analysis of waiting list demand, network utilisation and flexibility within a Network Area or Station.

=== Definitions

Definitions establish a common understanding of the key concepts used within this use case. They provide the agreed meaning of terms referenced in the Functional Requirements and associated information models.

Access Capacity Application (WaitingList.applicationReference)::
A request for additional transport capacity.

Waiting List Entry (WaitingList.applicationReference)::
An Access Capacity Application for which the requested capacity has not yet been allocated.

Requested Capacity (WaitingList.contractedCapacityConsumption, WaitingList.contractedCapacityFeedIn)::
The amount of transport capacity requested through an Access Capacity Application.

Consumption Capacity (WaitingList.contractedCapacityConsumption)::
Transport capacity intended for electricity consumption.

Production Capacity (WaitingList.contractedCapacityFeedIn)::
Transport capacity intended for electricity production.

Commissioning Date (WaitingList.commissioningDate)::
The expected date on which the requested capacity is required.

Congestion End Date (WaitingList.congestionEndDate)::
The date on which a network constraint is expected to be resolved.

Station (WaitingList.station, LoadProfile.station, FlexProfile.station)::
A network station used as a planning and assessment boundary.

Network Area (WaitingList.networkArea, FlexProfile.networkArea)::
A part of the electricity network for which capacity demand, flexibility and utilisation can be assessed.

Load Profile (LoadProfile.loadProfile)::
A representation of expected electricity consumption or production over time.

Profile Value (LoadProfile.measurementValue, LoadProfile.prognosisValue)::
The expected consumption or production value for a specific point in time.

Station Load Profile (LoadProfile.station, LoadProfile.measurementValue, LoadProfile.prognosisValue)::
A Load Profile representing the expected utilisation of a Station.

Network Utilisation (LoadProfile.measurementValue, LoadProfile.prognosisValue)::
The expected use of available transport capacity.

Autonomous Growth (LoadProfile.autonomousGrowth)::
Expected change in demand or production that is not directly related to an Access Capacity Application.

Flexibility (FlexProfile.contractType, FlexProfile.flexVolume)::
The capability to adjust electricity consumption or production.

Flexibility Contract (FlexProfile.contractId)::
An agreement defining the conditions under which Flexibility may be made available.

Flexibility Volume (FlexProfile.flexVolume)::
The amount of capacity that may be adjusted through Flexibility.

Contracted Capacity (WaitingList.contractedCapacityConsumption, WaitingList.contractedCapacityFeedIn)::
The capacity agreed between a Network Operator and a Connected Party.

Connected Party (WaitingList.connectionReference, FlexProfile.connectionReference)::
A party connected to the electricity network.

Allocation Scenario (Derived)::
An assessment combining Waiting List demand, Network Utilisation and Flexibility.

Study Area (WaitingList.networkArea, LoadProfile.station, FlexProfile.station)::
The geographic or electrical area for which an analysis is performed.

== Functional Requirements

Functional Requirements describe the information that shall be available to support the business objectives of this use case. They are technology-independent and specify what information needs to be provided, rather than how it is exchanged, stored or processed.
Terms defined in the <<_definitions>> section shall be represented in italics when used in <<_functional_requirements>>.

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
|ID |Requirement

|ACA-FR-001
|Information about _Waiting List Entries_ shall be available.

|ACA-FR-002
|A _Waiting List Entry_ shall include the _Requested Capacity_.

|ACA-FR-003
|A _Waiting List Entry_ shall indicate whether the _Requested Capacity_ relates to _Consumption Capacity_, _Production Capacity_ or both.

|ACA-FR-004
|A _Waiting List Entry_ shall include the _Commissioning Date_.

|ACA-FR-005
|A _Waiting List Entry_ shall be associated with a _Station_ or _Network Area_.

|ACA-FR-006
|Information about aggregated _Requested Capacity_ per _Station_ shall be available.

|ACA-FR-007
|Information about aggregated _Requested Capacity_ per _Network Area_ shall be available.

|ACA-FR-008
|Information about _Network Utilisation_ shall be available for a _Station_.

|ACA-FR-009
|_Network Utilisation_ information shall be derived from one or more _Station Load Profiles_.

|ACA-FR-010
|A _Station Load Profile_ shall contain _Profile Values_ associated with specific points in time.

|ACA-FR-011
|Information about expected periods of transport capacity scarcity shall be available.

|ACA-FR-012
|Information about the impact of _Autonomous Growth_ on _Network Utilisation_ shall be available.

|ACA-FR-013
|Information about available _Flexibility_ shall be available for a _Station_, _Network Area_ or _Connected Party_.

|ACA-FR-014
|_Flexibility_ information shall include a _Flexibility Volume_.

|ACA-FR-015
|_Flexibility_ information may include a reference to a _Flexibility Contract_.

|ACA-FR-016
|Information about aggregated _Flexibility Volume_ within a _Network Area_ shall be available.

|ACA-FR-017
|Information about available _Flexibility Volume_ for specific points in time shall be available.

|ACA-FR-018
|Information shall be available to relate _Requested Capacity_ to expected _Network Utilisation_.

|ACA-FR-019
|Information shall be available to relate _Requested Capacity_ to available _Flexibility Volume_.

|ACA-FR-020
|Information shall be available to assess the contribution of _Flexibility_ to the accommodation of _Waiting List Entries_.

|ACA-FR-021
|Information shall be available to support the assessment of _Waiting List Entries_ within an _Allocation Scenario_.

|ACA-FR-022
|An _Allocation Scenario_ shall combine information about _Waiting List Entries_, _Network Utilisation_ and available _Flexibility_.

|ACA-FR-023
|Information about remaining transport capacity following an _Allocation Scenario_ shall be available.

|ACA-FR-024
|Information shall be available to identify _Waiting List Entries_ affected by transport constraints.

|ACA-FR-025
|Information shall be available to assess the effect of a _Congestion End Date_ on _Waiting List Entries_.

|===
